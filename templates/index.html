About SUMMORA

Autonomous AI Text Summarization Platform

Neural Network Architecture

SUMMORA is powered by a locally hosted, specialized T5 (Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer) conditional generation model. Trained with deep sequence-to-sequence bidirectional encoder and autoregressive decoder stages, the model synthesizes abstractive summaries that preserve critical facts, context, and semantic nuance without relying on external cloud APIs.

Document-Length Intelligent Chunking

To support comprehensive articles and multi-page research documents exceeding standard token position limits, SUMMORA incorporates recursive sentence-aware chunking. Texts are segmented along natural semantic boundaries, individually summarized, and smoothly synthesized to ensure complete document coverage without truncation.

Zero Cloud Leakage & Total Privacy

All inference executes 100% locally on your machine. No document contents, queries, or summaries are transmitted to external servers or third-party AI providers. Your sensitive data remains completely private and secure.
